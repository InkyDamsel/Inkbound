Fastest option: delete inside Inkbound

Sign in to the Inkbound account you want to remove, open Settings → Account, and choose Delete account. Inkbound requires fresh authentication and an exact confirmation phrase so an account cannot be deleted accidentally.

Request deletion without opening the app

If you cannot access Inkbound, email the support address below from the email address associated with your Inkbound account. Use the subject Inkbound account deletion request. We may ask you to verify ownership before deletion so another person cannot erase your account.

Request account deletion

What deletion removes

Account deletion is intended to remove your Inkbound authentication account and private Inkbound account data, including your library, sessions, profile, reader-authored memories and account media stored under your user account. Administrative records are minimized or removed as appropriate for the deletion workflow.

Subscriptions

Deleting an Inkbound account does not automatically cancel a subscription owned by an external payment provider or app store. If you have a paid subscription, manage or cancel it through the provider that billed you. Inkbound does not require you to keep your personal account or reading data merely because billing remains active elsewhere.
