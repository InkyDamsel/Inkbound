Delete your Inkbound account and data

Inkbound gives you a direct path to permanent account deletion. You can delete your account from inside Inkbound, or request deletion from Inkbound Support if you cannot access your account.

Last updated September 11, 2026

Account deletion is permanent. Once deletion finishes, your Inkbound login and live Inkbound account data cannot be restored through the app. If you want a copy first, use Inkbound's Complete Inkbound Backup or Library CSV export before deleting your account.

Delete your account from Inkbound

  1. Sign in to the Inkbound account you want to delete. Account deletion is available only to the authenticated account owner.
  2. Open Settings. Go to the account and device-data controls and choose the option to delete your account and data.
  3. Review the permanent-deletion warning. If you have a paid subscription, review the separate subscription notice before continuing.
  4. Complete the confirmations and verify your account again when requested. Inkbound may require recent authentication and the confirmation shown in the app to protect your account.
  5. Confirm deletion. Inkbound sends the request to its authenticated deletion service and removes the live Inkbound account data associated with your user ID.

What Inkbound deletes

Inkbound's account-deletion service removes active account records rather than merely disabling the login.

Account and identity records

  • Your Firebase Authentication account and Inkbound login.
  • Your Inkbound user-directory and profile record.
  • Your Inkbound entitlement and access record.
  • Live account records associated with your user ID that are part of the Inkbound account data tree.

Your reading archive

  • Your cloud library and user-owned book records.
  • Reading sessions, progress, reading history, and related derived account data.
  • Collections, tags, goals, preferences, memories, notes, and other records stored beneath your Inkbound user account.
  • Uploaded account media stored under your Inkbound user folder, including supported profile, memory, cover, and collection media.

Retention after deletion

Inkbound does not keep the deleted account active after the account-deletion service completes. The live Inkbound account records described above are removed as part of the deletion process.

Limited information may remain temporarily in infrastructure backups, security or operational logs, or records that must be preserved for legitimate legal, fraud-prevention, accounting, or security reasons. Inkbound does not use residual backup records to recreate a deleted account. Infrastructure-provider backup and log lifetimes are governed by the applicable Firebase and Google Cloud configuration and policies.

Payment, transaction, invoice, or subscription records held by Stripe, Google Play, or another billing provider are controlled by that provider and are not deleted by Inkbound's account-deletion function.

Subscriptions are managed separately

Deleting an Inkbound account does not automatically cancel a paid subscription managed by Stripe, Google Play, or another external billing provider. If you have a paid subscription, cancel it with the provider that manages it so future billing does not continue after your Inkbound account is removed.

If you only activated Inkbound's no-card Premium trial and did not separately purchase a subscription, there is no paid subscription created by that trial to cancel.

Can't access your account?

Email Inkbound Support from the email address associated with your Inkbound account and request permanent account deletion. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request. Do not send passwords, sign-in codes, or payment-card information by email.
