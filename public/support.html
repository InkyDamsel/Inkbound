Inkbound Support

For help with your account, library, imports, subscriptions, privacy, or the app, email inkbound@inkydameinteractive.com.

Before contacting support

  • If changes are not appearing on another device, keep Inkbound open and online for a little while so it can finish updating your library.
  • For import issues, include where the library came from and the counts shown in Import History, but do not email your password or sign-in codes.
  • For account deletion, use Inkbound Settings when possible or visit the Delete Account page.
  • For billing questions, tell us where you subscribed, such as Google Play, once paid subscriptions are available.
