Inkbound Support

For account, sync, import, subscription, privacy, or technical support, email inkbound@inkydameinteractive.com.

Before contacting support

  • For sync issues, keep Inkbound open while online long enough for pending changes to finish.
  • For import issues, include the import source and the counts shown in Import History, but do not email passwords or private authentication credentials.
  • For account deletion, use Inkbound Settings when possible or visit the Delete Account page.
  • For billing questions, include which provider or app store handled the purchase once paid subscriptions are available.
