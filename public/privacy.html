Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

Inkbound is a private personal reading journal. We collect and process only the information needed to provide your account, synchronize your reading archive, protect the service, and provide features you choose to use.

Information you provide

Depending on the features you use, Inkbound may store account information such as your email address and display name; your library, editions, reading status and progress; reading sessions, dates, durations, notes, quotes, reviews, collections, reading goals, memories and time capsules; profile information; and photos you choose to add to Photo Memories or your profile.

Information created by the service

Inkbound creates technical records needed to synchronize and secure your account, such as record identifiers, update timestamps, entitlement status, account verification status, and limited operational or administrative audit information. Reading statistics and insights are derived from the reading data in your account.

How information is used

We use account information to operate Inkbound, synchronize your reading archive between devices, calculate progress and statistics, provide requested features, recover data after reconnecting, prevent abuse, administer account access, and respond to support or deletion requests. We do not sell your personal reading data.

Cloud providers and third-party services

Inkbound uses Firebase and Google Cloud services for authentication, database storage, file storage, and server functions. Book metadata may be retrieved from external book-data providers when you search for or enrich a book. Those providers receive the search information required to answer the request.

If paid subscriptions are offered, payment processing is handled by the applicable payment or app-store provider. Inkbound should receive subscription and entitlement information needed to grant access, not your full payment-card details.

Photos and media

Photos you add are compressed for Inkbound use and stored privately under your authenticated account. Firebase Storage rules restrict account media to the authenticated owner. Device copies may be cached locally so the app can display your media efficiently and support offline use.

Data retention and deletion

Your account data is retained while your Inkbound account remains active, subject to ordinary service backups and legal requirements. You can delete your account from Inkbound Settings. Account deletion removes the Inkbound user data tree, account media, entitlement record, and authentication account. Subscription billing, if any, is managed separately by the provider that owns the subscription.

You may also request deletion from the public Delete Account page.

Security

Inkbound uses authenticated access controls and server-managed authorization for private account data and entitlements. No online service can promise absolute security, so we continuously work to limit access and reduce unnecessary data collection.

Children

Inkbound is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review and remove it as appropriate.

Changes

We may update this policy as Inkbound changes. Material changes will be reflected by updating the effective date and, when appropriate, providing notice within the service.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests can be sent to inkbound@inkydameinteractive.com.
